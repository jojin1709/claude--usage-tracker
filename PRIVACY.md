# Privacy Policy for Claude Usage Tracker

**Last Updated:** May 5, 2026

## 🛡️ Our Commitment to Your Privacy
The Claude Usage Tracker is designed with a "Privacy First" philosophy. This extension is a tool for transparency and productivity, not for data harvesting. We believe your AI interactions should remain private.

## 📊 Data Collection and Storage
The extension collects and stores only the minimum amount of data necessary to provide usage tracking functionality:

*   **Usage Metrics**: The number of tokens sent and messages remaining within your current Claude.ai session and weekly limits.
*   **Reset Timestamps**: The time when your usage limits are scheduled to reset.
*   **Organization ID**: A unique identifier for your Claude organization, used solely to ensure data is associated with the correct account context.
*   **Configuration Settings**: Your user-defined preferences (e.g., API key if provided for higher accuracy, notification toggles).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **All data is stored locally on your device** using your browser's secure storage API (`chrome.storage.local`). We do not maintain any central servers or databases to collect this information.

## 🚫 What We DO NOT Collect
*   **Conversation Content**: We do *not* read, store, or transmit the text of your prompts or Claude's responses.
*   **Personal Information**: We do *not* collect your name, email address, IP address, or any other personally identifiable information (PII).
*   **Cookies**: We do *not* track your cookies beyond reading the session identifiers required to authenticate with Claude's internal usage API.

## 🔄 Data Usage and Sharing
*   **Zero Data Sharing**: We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third parties.
*   **Functional Use Only**: Data is used exclusively within the extension to render usage bars, calculate forecasts, and provide real-time status badges.

## 🔒 Security
Since your data never leaves your browser, its security is tied to your browser's security. We use standard browser APIs to ensure that data is sandboxed and inaccessible to other websites or extensions.

## 👤 User Rights
You have full control over your data:
*   **View Data**: You can view the raw tracked data at any time via the "Debug Logs" page in the extension settings.
*   **Delete Data**: Uninstalling the extension will automatically purge all locally stored usage data.
